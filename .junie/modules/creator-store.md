# Creator Store

## Purpose
Phase CG-4 of `.junie/plans/business-competitor-gap.md`. A creator sells from their bio handle —
digital downloads, bookings, a tip jar and paid questions — and is **paid directly into their own
Stripe account at a 0% platform fee** (Principle 3: Alima's money comes from the subscription, not a
cut of a sale). It closes the "nothing to sell" half of the October 2026 competitor gap against
Planoly's Creator Store, Stan and Beacons.

The differentiator over all of them is CG-4.7: a product becomes an AI-drafted **launch collection**
the owner reviews and schedules.

**Two Stripe integrations, kept apart (Principle 4).** Our subscriptions use Cashier
(`config/cashier.php`, `plans.md`); creators' sales use Stripe Connect behind
`App\Contracts\PaymentProvider` with its own keys (`config/payments.php`), its own webhook endpoint
and secret, and its own tables. Nothing is shared — a delivery signed for one is rejected by the
other (covered by a test).

## Models / Tables
All created by `database/migrations/2026_11_20_100100_create_creator_store_tables.php`. Money is
**always minor units** (`*_minor`, pence/cents) — never a float, anywhere.

- `PaymentAccount` (`payment_accounts`): `org_id` **unique**, `provider`, `external_account_id`
  unique, `charges_enabled`, `payouts_enabled`, `requirements_due` json, `country`,
  `default_currency`. Holds **no secret** — only the account id Stripe gives us. `canSell()` is
  `charges_enabled` (payouts can still be pending; the money is the creator's either way).
- `Product` (`products`): `org_id`, `client_id`, `type` (`ProductType`), `title`, `description`,
  `price_minor`, `currency`, `status` (`ProductStatus`), `cover_asset_id`, `download_asset_id`
  (both `assets`, `nullOnDelete`), `settings` json. `settings` carries the per-type payload so a new
  type needs no migration: `download_limit`/`download_expiry_hours`, `availability` (bookings),
  `min_minor` (tips), `answer_window_hours` (Q&A).
- `Order` (`orders`): `org_id`, `product_id`, `coupon_id`, `buyer_email`, `buyer_name`,
  `amount_minor`, `currency`, `status` (`OrderStatus`), `external_session_id` **unique** (what makes
  a replayed `checkout.session.completed` a no-op), `external_payment_id`, `message` (a tip note or
  the question), `answer`, `authorised_until` (Q&A only), `paid_at`, `fulfilled_at`, `refunded_at`,
  `download_count`. The buyer is an email address, never a user: nobody signs up to buy.
- `OrderEvent` (`order_events`): append-only audit (`$timestamps = false`, `created_at` only) —
  every status change, link issued, download served, mismatch, refund.
- `Coupon` (`coupons`): `org_id` + `code` **unique together**, `product_id` (null = whole store),
  `percent_off` **or** `amount_off_minor`, `expires_at`, `max_redemptions`, `redemptions`.
- `Booking` (`bookings`): `order_id`, `product_id`, `starts_at`/`ends_at` **UTC**, `status`.
  `unique(product_id, starts_at)` is the real double-booking guard.
- `ConnectWebhookEvent` (`connect_webhook_events`): `stripe_event_id` unique — the Connect
  idempotency ledger, deliberately **not** Cashier's `stripe_webhook_events`.

## Enums
- `App\Enums\ProductType`: `download`, `booking`, `tip`, `qa`. Behaviour lives on the enum:
  `buyerSetsAmount()` (tips), `isAuthorisedThenCaptured()` (Q&A), `takesBuyerMessage()`.
- `App\Enums\ProductStatus`: `draft`, `live`, `archived`. Only `live` renders or takes a checkout.
- `App\Enums\OrderStatus`: `pending → paid → fulfilled → refunded`, with `cancelled`.
  `allowedNext()`/`canTransitionTo()` is the authority; `isPaid()` means the money is taken.
- `App\Enums\Feature::Store` — the plan gate (Creator Pro; see `plans.md`).

## Contracts / Services
- `App\Contracts\PaymentProvider` — `createConnectedAccount`, `onboardingLink`, `accountStatus`,
  `createCheckout`, `capture`, `cancelAuthorisation`, `refund`, `verifyWebhook`.
- `App\Services\Payments\StripeConnectProvider` — stripe-php (**not** Cashier). Every charge is a
  **direct charge on the connected account** (`Stripe-Account` header) with **no
  `application_fee_amount`**: the money never touches Alima's balance. A Q&A session is created with
  `capture_method: manual`. Failures are normalised to `PaymentProviderException`, whose
  `friendlyMessage()` is what the UI shows — the vendor's own wording never reaches a page.
- `App\Services\Payments\NullPaymentProvider` — dev/CI with no keys. Fakes the *shape* of checkout
  and **refuses every webhook**: with no signing secret there is nothing to verify, and waving
  deliveries through would be a hole in a money path.
- Bound in `AppServiceProvider` only when `payments.provider = stripe` **and** a Connect secret
  exists; otherwise the null provider.
- `App\Services\Store\BookingSlotService` — availability → offered slots, and `isBookable()` for the
  re-check at checkout. Slots are generated by the brand's **local wall clock** and then converted,
  like a post's `scheduled_at_utc`.

## Actions
- `StartConnectOnboardingAction` — creates the connected account on first run (inside a
  `lockForUpdate()` transaction, so two tabs can't open two Stripe accounts) and returns the hosted
  onboarding link. Checks `Feature::Store`.
- `SyncPaymentAccountAction` — reconciles the capability flags. `statusFromWebhookObject()` reads
  them straight off `account.updated`, so the webhook needs no API call; unknown fields fall back to
  "not enabled", never to "enabled".
- `CreateCheckoutAction` — every refusal happens here, before money moves (not on sale, account
  can't charge, amount under the card floor, coupon dead/out of scope, slot gone), each as a
  `CheckoutUnavailableException` the public page shows as a sentence. Claims the slot **and** the
  coupon redemption inside the transaction, so two buyers racing can't both win.
- `TransitionOrderStatusAction` — the only place a status changes; refuses anything off the map and
  writes the audit row in the same transaction.
- `MarkOrderPaidAction` — the webhook's side. Idempotent (a non-pending order is logged and left),
  and **verifies the session's amount and currency**: a mismatch records `payment.mismatch` and
  leaves the order pending for a human.
- `FulfilOrderAction` — delivery + receipt: a signed expiring download link, the booking (with
  `.ics` and a signed manage link), or nothing for a tip. A Q&A order is deliberately **not**
  fulfilled here.
- `AnswerQuestionAction` — the creator answering is what **captures** the Q&A payment. Refused once
  `authorised_until` has passed (the authorisation is gone by then). Capture first, then record.
- `RefundOrderAction` — provider first, status second, so a failed refund never looks done. Marking
  it refunded is also what revokes the download and frees the slot.
- `RescheduleBookingAction` — the buyer moving or cancelling their own booking from the signed link.
  Cancelling frees the slot and leaves the order alone: refunding is the creator's call.
- `ExpireStaleOrdersAction` (`store:expire-orders`, hourly) — cancels pending checkouts over an hour
  old (releasing their slot) and releases uncaptured Q&A authorisations past their window, well
  inside the ~7 days a card authorisation survives.
- `SaveProductAction` — create/update in one Action; gate-checked, whitelists `settings` per type,
  and drops an asset id that isn't this brand's.
- `GetStoreOverviewAction` — everything `/store` renders, in one read.
- `DraftLaunchCampaignAction` (CG-4.7) — a collection plus four **draft** posts (teaser, launch,
  reminder, social proof), captions written by the shared `RewriteCaptionAction`, so the brand-voice
  rewrite is already budget-checked, metered on `ai_usage_logs` and guardrailed, with our own seed
  copy as the fallback when AI is off or failing. Suggested slots come from `BestTimeService`.

## Routes / Controllers
Business shell (`workspace:business`; every mutation needs the brand-**update** ability, i.e.
Owner/Manager — money is not a Designer's or Viewer's to move):
- `GET /store` → `Business\StoreController@index`; `POST /store/setup` → hosted onboarding
  (`Inertia::location`); `GET /store/return` → sync on the way back from Stripe.
- `POST|PUT|DELETE /store/products[/{product}]` → `Business\ProductController` ("delete" archives, so
  order history keeps pointing at what was bought).
- `POST /store/coupons`, `DELETE /store/coupons/{coupon}` → same controller.
- `POST /store/orders/{order}/refund`, `POST /store/orders/{order}/answer` → `StoreController`.
- `POST /store/products/{product}/launch` → `StoreController@launch` (CG-4.7).

Public, on the bio handle (guest, `throttle:public-token`):
- `GET /@{handle}/p/{product}`, `POST /@{handle}/p/{product}/checkout`,
  `GET /@{handle}/thanks/{order}`, `POST /@{handle}/thanks/{order}/resend` (throttled 6/min)
  → `Store\CheckoutController`.

Signed-link only (`['signed', 'throttle:public-token']`) — the signature **is** the authorisation:
- `GET /store/download/{order}` → `Store\DownloadController`.
- `GET|POST /store/booking/{order}[/move|/cancel]` → `Store\BookingController`.

Webhook: `POST /webhooks/stripe-connect` → `Store\ConnectWebhookController` (public, CSRF-exempt via
the existing `webhooks/*` pattern, `throttle:webhooks`). Handles `account.updated`,
`checkout.session.completed` and `charge.refunded`; everything else is acknowledged and ignored.

Frontend: `pages/business/store.tsx`, `components/store/product-form.tsx`, `pages/public/product.tsx`,
`pages/public/order-thanks.tsx`, `pages/public/booking.tsx`. Nav: **Shop**, its own business chapter
(`09`, `/store`) — `accounts`/`team`/`settings` renumbered to 10–12. Order and product statuses are
`StatusPill` kinds `order` and `product` in `resources/js/lib/status.ts`.

## Business Rules
- **0% platform fee.** No `application_fee_amount`, ever. `payments.platform_fee_percent` is 0 and
  exists so the one place that would have to change is named, not so it can be turned up quietly.
- **The creator is the merchant.** Buyers pay them; Alima never holds the funds, and payout schedule
  questions go to Stripe (the page links out rather than inventing an answer).
- **No card data on our side.** The card is only ever entered on the provider's hosted page.
- **Selling is a plan gate** (`Feature::Store`, Creator Pro). The page renders without it and
  explains what upgrading unlocks, like `/bio`.
- **Only a `live` product on a published bio page is buyable.** Archiving a product takes it off the
  page by itself (the bio block resolves to nothing and stops rendering).
- **A paid question is authorised, not charged.** Captured when the creator answers; released if
  they don't. The window is capped at 144 hours by validation.
- **A tip's amount is the buyer's**, floored at the product's minimum; everything else is charged
  the product's price. The submitted amount is never trusted for a non-tip.
- **Stripe's charge floor** (`Product::MIN_CHARGE_MINOR`, 100 minor units) is enforced before
  checkout and after a coupon, so a 100%-off code can't produce a charge Stripe refuses.
- **Downloads are streamed through our route**, never the file's storage URL, so every hit is
  counted and the expiry, use limit and refund check all still apply to a link sitting in an inbox.
  Step GTM-1.1: streamed with `AssetStorage::readStream()`, not read into memory, so the same route
  serves a 500MB file from S3.
- **Not every file may be sold** (Step GTM-1.1, `Asset::downloadRefusalReason()`): the file must be
  on a **private** disk and its bytes must have been virus-scanned on the way in
  (`AssetSource::isVirusScanned()` — a web/MCP upload was, a Canva/Drive/Dropbox/URL import was
  not). Checked when the product goes live, so the creator finds out, **and** on every download hit,
  so a product that went live before the rule existed is still never served. The buyer's 410 never
  says which rule it was; the reason is logged.
- **Refund revokes delivery**: `Order::canDownload()` is false from that moment and the booking row
  is deleted.
- **An abandoned checkout holds its slot and coupon use** until `store:expire-orders` releases them,
  and releasing it is also what sends the abandoned-checkout email (Step GTM-2.2) — so a completed
  order can never get one, because it never reaches that sweep.
- **The buyer joins the creator's list at checkout, not at fulfilment** (Step GTM-2.1): an
  abandoned checkout needs a contact to email too. Buying is **not** consent to marketing — only
  the `marketing_opt_in` box on the checkout form sets it. See `audience.md`.

## Known Gaps / Open Questions

### 🚩 LAUNCH BLOCKER — Step GTM-0.3, payments/tax/entity (undecided as of 2026-11-07)

CG-0.2 was never answered and the store shipped anyway. **The store must not take money from a real
customer until all five of these are answered in writing.** No `STRIPE_PRICE_*` exists and the
super-admin billing switch defaults off, so nothing is live today — that is the only thing currently
preventing it, and it is not a deliberate guard. Reviewed and deliberately left open at GTM-0, since
answering 1–4 needs an accountant, not a developer.

1. **Which legal entity holds the Stripe platform account** (UK or UAE), for Cashier *and* for
   Connect. Decides where our subscription revenue and the platform relationship sit.
2. **Connect account type.** Express is the recommendation on file: Stripe-hosted onboarding and
   dashboard, least compliance surface for us. Not yet confirmed.
3. **Sales tax / VAT on creators' sales.** Either the creator's own responsibility with Stripe Tax
   on their connected account (the Planoly/Stan model, which is what the code assumes today), or a
   merchant-of-record partner (Fourthwall/Gumroad model). **Get an accountant's view on UK/EU VAT
   for digital goods before choosing** — digital goods to EU consumers are the risk, not the UK.
4. **Launch currencies for creator sales** (GBP + USD minimum; AED if Dubai matters early).
5. **Whether the free plan can sell, and at what fee.** (Today it cannot: `Feature::Store` is off
   on every gate of `business_free`, so this is a decision to make, not a hole to plug.) Beacons charges 9%, Linktree 12%, Planoly
   has no free web plan. If free selling is allowed, the fee goes in `config/plans.php` as a per-plan
   value — not a constant — and is enforced in the `PaymentProvider` implementation, with a test per
   plan fee.

When answered: record the answers here, mark CG-0.2 and GTM-0.3 done, and update CG-4's steps where
an answer changes them.

### Other gaps
- **S3 is one ops step away, not a code gap** (Step GTM-1.1). The download path goes through
  `AssetStorage` end to end and streams whatever disk is configured, and a file on the `public` disk
  is now refused outright, so CG-4.3's "paid downloads must not be served from local disk" is
  enforced in code. What remains is deployment, and it is **not done**:
  `composer require league/flysystem-aws-s3-v3`, `ASSETS_DISK=s3` and the `AWS_*` vars (see
  `config/assets.php`). Until then paid files sit on the private `local` disk — served only through
  the signed route, but on the app server's own volume.
- **A download's file is picked by media id**, typed into the product form, because there is no asset
  picker component to reuse yet. Fine for one creator; replace with a picker when one exists.
- **No Stripe Tax and no VAT handling** (plan §5.2 is still open): the creator is the merchant of
  record and responsible for their own tax. Nothing in the UI claims otherwise, and nothing invoices
  the buyer beyond the receipt.
- **Earnings are summed from our own orders**, not from Stripe's balance, so a charge created outside
  Alima isn't counted. Payouts are not mirrored at all.
- **Tax/receipt numbering, partial refunds and multi-item carts** are deliberately absent.
- **An abandoned checkout spends a coupon redemption** until the hourly command cancels the order;
  the redemption is not given back on cancellation (`shortcut: a cancelled order keeps its
  redemption, upgrade when a creator complains about a code running out`).
- **B-13.1 (abuse/cost safeguards) and B-13.2 (export/deletion)** are the plan's prerequisites for
  real money. Checkout is throttled and the webhook is signed and idempotent, but card-testing
  defence beyond Stripe's own, and export/deletion covering `products`/`orders`/`order_events`/
  `coupons`/`bookings`/`payment_accounts`, are still to do.
- **Bookings have no per-day cap, no holidays and no multi-length slots**; availability is one weekly
  window. A conflict with the creator's own calendar isn't checked (no calendar integration exists).
- No Stripe price/product objects are created: each checkout uses inline `price_data`, so a creator's
  Stripe dashboard shows the sale but not a catalogue.

## Gotchas
- **Never point the Connect env vars at Cashier's.** `STRIPE_CONNECT_SECRET` /
  `STRIPE_CONNECT_WEBHOOK_SECRET` are separate from `STRIPE_SECRET` / `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` on
  purpose; sharing them would break the isolation the plan's Principle 4 asks for (and the test that
  proves it).
- **Invokable Actions injected as properties need `($this->action)(…)`.** `$this->transition(…)` is
  a method call and fails at runtime, which is how it first broke here.
- **The success URL needs the order id**, which only exists after the order is created, so
  `CreateCheckoutAction` takes the bio `handle` and builds both return URLs itself.
- The checkout success page can load **before** the webhook arrives; it says "confirming" rather than
  claiming the sale, and the email is the real delivery.
