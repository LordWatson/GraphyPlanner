# Why stories with links are posted by hand

Most of what you schedule in Alima publishes itself. One kind of post can't: an Instagram story
with a **link, a poll, a mention, a hashtag or a location sticker**.

## The short version

Instagram only lets a person add links and stickers to a story, from inside the Instagram app. No
scheduling tool can add them for you, and a story can't be edited once it's live. So Alima gets
everything ready and tells the right person the moment it's time. They do the last few taps.

A plain story, with no link and no stickers, still publishes itself as it always has.

## What happens

1. **You plan it as usual.** Set the channel's format to Story, then add what you need under
   **Story extras**: the link and its sticker text, a mention, a poll, and a note on where to put
   them. The channel now reads **Needs posting by hand**.
2. **You choose who posts it.** Your team (the person the post is assigned to) or the client. You
   can set a default for each client on their page.
3. **The client approves it.** The link and the sticker text are listed beside the preview, so they
   are approved along with the creative.
4. **At the scheduled time, the poster's phone tells them.** "Time to post." By push, email, and
   WhatsApp if they've switched it on. No password needed.
5. **They tap Share to Instagram.** Alima copies the link and passes the picture or video to
   Instagram's story editor.
6. **In Instagram they add the link sticker, paste, place it, and share.**
7. **Back in Alima they tap I've posted it.** The post is marked as published.

Everything else on the same post goes out by itself, as normal.

## Good to know

- **Get the reminder on your phone.** Open Settings, then Notifications, and switch on story
  reminders on the phone you'll post from.
- **If nobody confirms.** After 15 minutes the poster gets one nudge. After 2 hours the story is
  marked as not posted and appears in Needs attention. From the post you can send a new reminder,
  or mark it as posted if it did go out.
- **If you'd rather it went out anyway.** On the story's extras you can choose "If no one posts it
  within 2 hours, publish it without the extras". It's off unless you turn it on, for that one
  post. Careful: if someone posts it by hand and forgets to confirm, it goes out twice.
- **Posting several in one sitting.** Stories to post lists every story that's waiting on you.

## What Alima can't do

- It can't add the sticker. The poster does, in Instagram.
- It can't check where the sticker was placed, or that the right link was pasted.
- It can't see Instagram, so "posted" is the poster's word for it. That also means a story posted
  by hand has no engagement numbers in your reports: they show as not available, not as zero.

## Why we don't work around it

Tools that claim to post link stickers for you do it by logging in to Instagram as you, or by
driving the app with a robot. Both break Instagram's rules and put the account at risk. Alima never
does either, and never asks for an Instagram password.
